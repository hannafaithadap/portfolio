About Me

Since 2021, I have been a dedicated freelancer and virtual professional, helping businesses and creators grow their online presence through social media management, graphic design, content creation, and administrative support.

I’ve had the privilege of collaborating with talented creators and businesses across the Philippines, the United States, and Australia. Every project is a chance to bring a thoughtful mix of creativity, strategy, and care to the people behind a brand.
